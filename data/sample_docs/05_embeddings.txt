An embedding is a numeric vector representation of an object, such as a sentence or document chunk. Neural embedding models map semantically related texts to vectors that are often nearby under a suitable similarity measure. Tokenization converts raw text into model input tokens, while the embedding model produces a vector that summarizes some aspects of meaning. Cosine similarity compares the angle between two vectors and ranges from minus one to one. After normalizing vectors to unit length, their dot product equals cosine similarity. For a small dataset, vectors can be kept in memory and searched with NumPy. Larger systems may use a vector database to index millions of chunks efficiently. Embedding model selection depends on languages, domain, retrieval benchmarks, available memory, and latency requirements.
